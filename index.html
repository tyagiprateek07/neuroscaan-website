Core Capabilities

Powered by Breakthrough Science

01

EEG Signal Processing

Non-invasive electroencephalogram analysis using 256-channel neural caps captures brain wave patterns with millisecond precision. Our AI decodes complex neural oscillations that differentiate typical and atypical development trajectories.

  • ✦ Real-time neural oscillation mapping
  • ✦ Frequency band anomaly detection
  • ✦ Cross-hemispheric connectivity analysis
02

Genomic Risk Profiling

Integrating polygenic risk scores with epigenetic markers, NeuroScaan identifies genetic predispositions across 1,000+ ASD-associated loci. Combined with environmental factor analysis for a holistic risk assessment.

  • ✦ Multi-loci genetic variant scanning
  • ✦ Epigenetic methylation patterns
  • ✦ Gene-environment interaction modeling
03

Smart Eye Tracking

Proprietary gaze-pattern analysis measures social attention, joint attention responses, and visual preference patterns. Infants naturally engage with our gamified screening — no special equipment beyond a tablet.

  • ✦ Saccade & fixation pattern analysis
  • ✦ Social vs. geometric preference mapping
  • ✦ Attention disengagement metrics
The Process

How NeuroScaan Works

A seamless four-step journey from initial screening to personalized care plan.

1

Upload & Intake

Parents upload developmental milestone videos and complete a smart questionnaire. Our AI begins preliminary behavioral analysis immediately.

2

Neural Analysis

Multi-modal AI processes EEG data, eye-tracking patterns, speech prosody, and motor coordination through our trained neural networks.

3

Spectrum Report

A comprehensive, easy-to-understand diagnostic report with spectrum positioning, confidence intervals, and development area breakdowns.

4

Care Pathway

Personalized intervention roadmap with specialist matching, therapy schedules, progress milestones, and community support group connections.
